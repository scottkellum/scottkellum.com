In January 2024, my wife and I bought our home in Los Angeles, one of the most expensive cities in the United States. We had an apartment we loved, but with two kids, we outgrew our one-bedroom apartment, and a 14% rent hike increased the urgency to move. Refusing to get priced out of the neighborhood we love, we crunched the numbers and decided to buy.

When researching home prices, the single-family home is the standard. In a city, these homes are prohibitively expensive. In my opinion, single-family homes shouldn’t exist in cities and therefore should be prohibitively expensive. Cities thrive on density. In my neighborhood, a single-family home starts at around $1.2M. We examined condos.
